Sidequest Audio Local Automation

A private tool used by Sidequest Audio to prepare its own YouTube videos. It runs on the channel owner's computer and isn't offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API to fill in the title, description, tags, settings, thumbnail, captions and playlist of videos on the Sidequest Audio channel. It shows every change and asks the owner before sending it.
