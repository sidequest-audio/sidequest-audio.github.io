Privacy policy

Sidequest Audio Local Automation. Last updated 25 November 2026.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Sidequest Audio YouTube channel. It has no other users and collects nothing from visitors, viewers or the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses YouTube API Services to read and update the owner's own channel name, videos, captions, thumbnails and playlists. It doesn't read comments, viewer information or analytics.

What it stores

On the owner's computer: the Google sign-in token, the channel's name and ID, the IDs of the channel's videos, and checksums of the last thumbnail and captions it uploaded. The video IDs and checksums may also be kept in the project's code repository. The sign-in token is not.

Sharing

Data goes only to Google, to make the changes the owner approves. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in Google security settings and delete the stored files from the computer.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. The YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

Contact

Contact Sidequest Audio through its YouTube channel.
